Comparative Social Network Ecology

A Central Hub For Animal Social Network Databases

The Comparative Social Network Ecology (CSNE) initiative is a central hub for existing social network databases. While CSNE does not host data directly, it provides a catalogue of existing databases, making it easy to see what data is available for cross-species comparative research and connect with the research groups who manage them.
